      Field notes

      These notes scroll vertically inside their own region. Focus the notes and use Up/Down or Page Up/Page Down. Tab leaves the region; links inside it remain separate stops.

      1. Before choosing a route, discuss the distance, surfaces and planned rest stops with everyone taking part. Make a plan that works for the whole group.

      2. Confirm current access information with the venue. The table is illustrative and cannot describe temporary closures, weather damage or every individual access need.

      3. Allow time to pause and record observations. A shorter route with useful rest points may support the survey better than covering every possible area.

      4. Keep a clear return plan and agree how the group will communicate if someone needs to stop. Check transport arrangements before setting out.

      5. Bring the information in a format everyone can use. Inspect guide-format and native input values in the related form recipe.

      6. Leave space for follow-up notes. Read the form integration guidance before adding saving, uploads or asynchronous requests to this local example.
